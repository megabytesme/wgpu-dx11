## Ordered implementation and test gates

1. Establish exact FL9_3 selection, current Level9_1 execution and both FXC Level9_3 production profiles. Execute static/dynamic PS branches, derivative and gradient output and dependent texture lookups, and verify disassembly against the expanded instruction, temp-register and flow budgets. Drive data-dependent VS branches at the disputed native/FXC boundary and pin the observed outcome before changing a cross-stage flag. Use FL9_2 device creation of Level9_3 bytecode as a negative control.
2. Produce four simultaneous color targets with distinct stored colors, independent masks and common blend enable, plus D24S8. Validate zero and five targets as applicable, incompatible target bit widths and mixed blend enable/equation states. Match clear, load/store and readback per target. Gate general `INDEPENDENT_BLEND` behind full semantics. Preserve the FL9 sRGB blend restrictions.
3. Use native `DrawIndexedInstanced` with slot zero per-vertex, indexed U16/U32 and two instanced streams, nonzero first instance, restart and base vertex. Check native draw with no per-instance data used by shader, illegal slot arrangement and unsupported native nonindexed instanced draw. Prove any public fallback lowering independently. Test half-float x2/x4 IA formats and verify offset, stride and overlap.
4. Create 4096 2D axes and 4096 cube edge, exercise all six faces and last mip with unique values, then probe size + 1 on suitable allocations and invalid cube shapes. Keep volume and binding inheritance. Exercise repeat 8,192, ordinary supported border colors, finite MaxLOD/fractional MinLOD, RG32/RGBA32, and RGBA16F blending where the format operation is required. Distinguish wrong format/view and NPOT compatibility behavior from successful optional support.
5. If the runtime reports D3D11.1 CB options, verify windowed binding, offsets, partial updates and non-overlapping state after copy or reuse. Check inherited occlusion, depth/stencil and real suspend/resume, including command-order failures, before certifying the exact signed UWP packages.

## Native/public blockers

Arbitrary native instance step divisors, native float4 sampler border color and D3D9 legacy driver capability exposure remain literal gaps. Public nonindexed instancing needs a proven equivalent lowering. Independent FL9_3 masks with common blend enable do not satisfy the general independent blend flag. Record unresolved per-stage VS flow and runtime sampler limits as blockers where they affect a mandatory operation.
