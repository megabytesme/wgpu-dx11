# FL9_1 through FL9_3 research

Source audit of wgpu at `aa7b65c43ef008d0a92d20fcdf9f10fa0883f157`. Each tier is **INCOMPLETE**. This research packet adds no hardware runs. Historical results below are limited to the named device, driver, requested feature level, operation, and revision recorded in the original evidence.

## Native limits and restrictions

Values in the texture dimension rows are the portable advertised limits. For 2D, implementations can accept a larger dimension. A successful larger native allocation is an observed extension, while the conservative public limit can still reject it. Boundary tests should use thin legal POT resources where practical to avoid conflating an address limit with memory pressure.

All three tiers conditionally support 2D NPOT with one mip and no native wrap, unless full support is queried. The baseline texture shape is one ordinary 2D layer or exactly six cube faces. The SRV must include the smallest mip and all layers of its resource. Baseline depth attachments have one mip. Sampled textures are single-sample under the baseline device methods. RTVs require 2D resources and clearing a multi-face native RTV affects its first slice only. For DEFAULT or IMMUTABLE 2D/3D resources, applicable binds cannot be empty. Native buffers have one of vertex/index/constant roles and no RTV. GPU-to-GPU copies are restricted to 2D resources and buffers, and volume or SRV-only GPU-to-CPU and mipmapped-volume region copies have additional restrictions. Use positive and negative native descriptors for each case, followed by public validation and pixel/byte comparisons for the corresponding wgpu operation. Match native restrictions on a baseline capability device even when a modern adapter offers a positive optional query. [Device methods](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/direct3d11/d3d11-graphics-reference-10level9-device), [context methods](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/direct3d11/d3d11-graphics-reference-10level9-context).

The resource-limit header lists 1D dimensions, while D3D11 texture creation expressly disallows 1D in 10Level9. Lack of native ordinary 2D arrays, cube arrays, vertex texture binds, UAVs, compute, indirect drawing, predication, stream output, geometry/tessellation, indexed point lists and adjacency modes are tier boundaries. Public emulation for an individual operation needs its own equivalence test. Native rasterizer requires depth clipping and zero bias clamp. FL9_1 also requires a nonzero native OM sample mask. All three prohibit alpha-to-coverage, source-alpha-saturate and dual-source blending. The output-merger format restrictions at FL9_3 allow independent color masks, with common enable, and these need a narrower capability than wgpu's general independent blend guarantee. Keep driver D3D9 caps such as fullscreen gamma and two-sided stencil separately identified from the D3D11 application contract. [Feature-level table](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/direct3d11/overviews-direct3d-11-devices-downlevel-intro), [device methods](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/direct3d11/d3d11-graphics-reference-10level9-device), [context methods](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/direct3d11/d3d11-graphics-reference-10level9-context), [driver caps](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/display/required-direct3d-9-capabilities).

The driver capability table changes MaxVertexW from 0 to 1e10 at FL9_2 and specifies the driver event and occlusion callback values. FL9_3 adds 256 driver VS constants and legacy VS 3.0 caps to qualify for instancing. Keep the driver requirements recorded independently from API-exposed operations. Legacy fixed-function shading and Direct3D 9 shader-model-3 profiles have no matching D3D11 FL9 public entry point. Ordinary CTS validates public semantics. It cannot establish driver format bits, FXC profile limits, native event/GenerateMips operations, exact native permits/rejects, WDDM, platform lifecycle, or physical device-recovery behavior.

Its optional first-adapter selection is insufficient for exact FL9_2/3 certification.
- Certification stays **INCOMPLETE** while any mandatory native case, equivalent public operation, Core/HAL negative, physical result, lower-tier regression, or literal native/public API gap is open.
